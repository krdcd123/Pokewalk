# Bundled sprites (optional)

Put your sprites here before building, and they're baked into the app:

- `front/`: Gen 5 animated sprites, e.g. `25.gif`, `025.gif` or `pikachu.gif`
- `shiny/`: shiny animated sprites, named the same way
- `icons/`: party/box icons, used on the Watch, map pins and the PC, e.g. `25.png`
- `items/`: item sprites, e.g. `poke-ball.png`, `great-ball.png`, `ultra-ball.png`, `rare-candy.png`, `fire-stone.png`

You don't have to bundle them. You can also import a folder from the app (**Settings › Sprites**), or copy files into **Files › On My iPhone › PokéWalk › Sprites**. Anything missing is downloaded from PokeAPI's sprite repo.
